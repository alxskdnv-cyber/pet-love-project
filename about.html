About

Why animals mean so much to me

Animals have a way of creating connection without needing words.

I have always loved animals, but living with my own pets made that connection much deeper. They bring structure, joy, affection and personality into everyday life.

Daisy and Bublik are very different, but both constantly remind me how much responsibility and care goes into giving an animal a happy life.

I created this project as a way to combine something personal with everything I have learned about responsive web design.

Kindness
